How does it work? The rules & the mathematics

The host’s promise

The car is placed randomly. After your first pick, the host opens an unchosen door with a goat, then always offers you a switch. If either goat door is available, the host chooses randomly.

Why switching wins more often

Your first pick has a 1 in 3 chance of being the car. Staying wins only when that first pick was right. Switching wins whenever your first pick was wrong: 2 out of 3 times, on average.

Expected win rates: switch ≈ 66.7%; stay ≈ 33.3%. Small samples can look very different. Try both strategies over many rounds.
